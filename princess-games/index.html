Princess Games: Phoenix's Castle

Visit Kitty Princess Phoenix Sparkles in her 3D castle. Dress her up, light the windows with your magic wand, and throw a royal tea party.

Welcome to my castle! I'm Princess Phoenix Sparkles. Tap the buttons above to play!

How to play: walk with the joystick, arrow keys or WASD. Drag to look around. Turn on Magic wand and tap the castle windows to light them all. Tap Phoenix, the kitties, and the family in the garden to say hi.

For grown-ups:Puts your child's face on the visiting prince or princess. The photo stays on this device.

A princess game that's kind, bright and a little bit magic

Kitty Princess Phoenix Sparkles lives in a pink castle with glowing towers, a sparkling moat, and two kitty best friends named Marshmallow and Sugarplum. This free 3D princess game mixes princess dress up with gentle learning: counting teacups at the royal tea party, finding every window to light, and saying hello to the whole family in the garden.

Want more? Read the Sparkles Family bedtime stories, or head to Kids World for colors, shapes and letters.

Castle questions

Who is Kitty Princess Phoenix Sparkles?

She is an original Comics Come Alive character: a kind cat-eared princess with a five-point pearl crown who loves tea parties, twirling and helping her friends.

Is this princess dress up game free?

Yes. The castle, dress up, tea party and magic wand games are all free to play in your browser with no sign-up.

What ages is the princess castle for?

It is made for kids around 2 to 8. Little ones can explore and tap, and older kids can count the teacups and light every window.
