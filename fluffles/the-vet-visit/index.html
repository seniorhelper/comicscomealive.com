The Vet Visit

Fluffles is off to the vet for a checkup, and everything is new: the carrier, the table, the cold stethoscope. A gentle story about being brave.

At the vet...

Later, at home...

Fluffles Fact

A guinea pig's front teeth never stop growing! Chewing lots of hay keeps them worn down, and a yearly vet checkup helps keep teeth healthy.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. Fluffles peeks out of a pet carrier, a little nervous.

    Fluffles: Where are we going?

  2. Panel 2. On the exam table, Fluffles sweats as a stethoscope comes near. The friendly vet speaks from off panel.

    The vet: Hello, Fluffles! I am the vet. I just want to say hi to your heart.

  3. Panel 3. The vet gently checks Fluffles, who relaxes and purrs.

    The vet: Good heart. Shiny coat. Now, say AHHH!

    Sound: purr

  4. Panel 4. Close-up of Fluffles grinning to show two neat little front teeth.

    The vet: Perfect teeth! All that hay keeps them just the right size.

  5. Panel 5. Fluffles is amazed to learn their teeth never stop growing.

    Fluffles: My teeth keep GROWING? Forever?!

  6. Panel 6. Back home, Fluffles dives into the hay to keep those teeth trim. Biscuit is proud.

    Fluffles: Home! Must chew hay. For SCIENCE.

    Biscuit: Brave buddy.
