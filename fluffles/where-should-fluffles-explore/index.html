Where Should Fluffles Explore?

Floor time! You choose where curious Fluffles explores first: the cardboard castle, the fleece mountain, or the sneaky gap behind the couch.

Where should Fluffles explore first?

You choose! Tap a button to see what happens.

If you pick the cardboard castle...

Great pick! Plain cardboard makes a perfect hideout.

If you pick the fleece mountain...

Great pick! Soft fleece is cozy and safe to burrow in.

If you pick the gap behind the couch...

Nope, not that one. Gaps and cords are not safe. Floor time belongs in a guinea-pig-proofed space.

Fluffles Fact

Guinea pigs chew almost everything, including electrical cords. Floor time should always be supervised, in a safe space with no wires or gaps to squeeze into.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.
