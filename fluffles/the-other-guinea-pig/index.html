The Other Guinea Pig

Fluffles meets a mysterious black-and-white guinea pig who copies everything. A silent, old-school newspaper strip with a sweet ending.

Flufflesby Comics Come AliveNo. 14 · a silent strip

Fluffles Fact

A guinea pig's eyes are on the sides of its head, so it can see almost all the way around, but not right under its own nose. Its whiskers help it feel what is close by.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. During floor time, Fluffles discovers a mirror and sees another black-and-white guinea pig. Both look startled.

  2. Panel 2. Fluffles tilts their head. The other guinea pig tilts too.

  3. Panel 3. Fluffles popcorns. The other guinea pig popcorns at exactly the same time.

  4. Panel 4. Fluffles brings Biscuit to the mirror. Now there are two new friends in it, and everybody is happy.
