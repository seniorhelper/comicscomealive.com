The First Snowflake

A silent comic with no words at all. Fluffles sees snow for the very first time through the window, and wonder does all the talking.

A silent comic: no words, just pictures. Scroll slowly and let them talk.

Fluffles Fact

Guinea pigs are indoor pets. They are most comfortable around 60 to 80 degrees F (16 to 27 C) and should be kept away from cold drafts, heaters and hot sun.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. Fluffles sits indoors near a window. The sky outside is gray and soft.

  2. Panel 2. Close-up through the window: one big, lacy snowflake drifts down.

  3. Panel 3. Fluffles faces us, eyes sparkling with wonder.

  4. Panel 4. Lots of snowflakes now. Fluffles stands up on back legs at the window, nose almost touching the glass. Biscuit watches too.

  5. Panel 5. Warm inside, Fluffles and Biscuit snuggle on a fleece blanket and fall asleep while snow falls outside.
