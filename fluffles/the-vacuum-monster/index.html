The Vacuum Monster

A roaring purple monster is prowling the living room! Fluffles bravely... hides. A classic four-panel daily about being brave in your own way.

Flufflesby Comics Come AliveNo. 7

© 2026 Comics Come Alive

Fluffles Fact

Guinea pigs startle at loud noises. When they are scared they may freeze or dash to a hiding spot, so give them a calm, quiet place to live.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. A loud vacuum cleaner roars across the floor. Fluffles stares in alarm.

    Sound: VRRRRMMM

  2. Panel 2. Only two worried eyes are visible inside the dark doorway of the hidey house.

    Fluffles: I am being VERY brave in here.

  3. Panel 3. Silence. A gentle hand offers a leaf toward the hidey house.

    The Tall One: All done! It's quiet now.

  4. Panel 4. Fluffles proudly munches the leaf. Biscuit, unimpressed, points out the truth.

    Fluffles: I scared it away.

    Biscuit: You were under the hay.
