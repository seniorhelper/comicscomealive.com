Picnic Pick

It is a floor-time picnic and Fluffles gets to choose a snack. You decide which one! Some picks are great and some are a big "nope." Can you choose wisely?

Which snack should Fluffles pick?

Fluffles Fact

Some foods are never safe for guinea pigs, like chocolate, onions, garlic, avocado and potatoes. Always check with a vet or a trusted care guide before sharing a new food.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.
