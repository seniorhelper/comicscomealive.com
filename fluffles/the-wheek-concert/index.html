The Wheek Concert

Click goes the fridge, and the loudest concert in the house begins! A bright, old-school animated comic about the famous guinea pig wheek.

Tap the panel or press Next to turn the page. Arrow keys work too.

Fluffles Fact

Guinea pigs learn the sounds of their daily routine. Many wheek loudly when they hear the fridge open or a veggie bag rustle, because snack time might be coming.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. Both guinea pigs freeze, ears up, at the click of the fridge door.

    Caption: Somewhere in the kitchen, the fridge door opens.

    Sound: CLICK

  2. Panel 2. They throw back their heads and wheek in a loud duet. Music notes fly.

    Fluffles (shouting): WHEEEK!

    Biscuit (shouting): WHEEEEEK!

  3. Panel 3. The doorbell rings. The guinea pigs wonder if it means snacks.

    Fluffles: Was that the snack door?

    Biscuit: Every door is the snack door.

    Sound: DING DONG

  4. Panel 4. The Tall One arrives with fresh greens and a slice of pepper.

    The Tall One: Okay, okay! The concert worked!

  5. Panel 5. Quiet munching. Fluffles whispers hopefully for an encore.

    Sound: munch

    Fluffles (whispering): ...encore?
