The Hay Fort

A giant hay delivery means one thing: it is fort time! Fluffles and Biscuit build the greatest hay fort ever... and then things get crunchy.

The Adventures ofFluffles
The Hay Fort

Fluffles Fact

Hay should make up most of a guinea pig's diet and be available all day long. It is great for their tummies and their teeth.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. A mountain of fresh hay. Fluffles has a big idea.

    Fluffles: Let's build a FORT!

  2. Panel 2. Building begins. Biscuit carries hay, stomping it into walls.

    Sound: STOMP STOMP

  3. Panel 3. The finished hay fort, with a leaf flag on top. Fluffles peeks out of the top.

    Fluffles: Fort Fluffles! Nobody gets in without the password.

  4. Panel 4. Biscuit knocks. A voice from inside the fort answers.

    Biscuit: Password?

    Fluffles: HAY.

  5. Panel 5. Both are inside the fort... eating the walls.

    Sound: munch munch munch

  6. Panel 6. The fort is completely gone. Two round, happy guinea pigs sit where it used to be.

    Caption: Fort Fluffles: delicious, but short-lived.

    Fluffles: We need more fort.
