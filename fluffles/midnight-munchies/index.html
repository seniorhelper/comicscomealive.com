Midnight Munchies

It is 3 a.m. and Fluffles is wide awake and hungry. But why is Biscuit snoring with both eyes open? A moonlit vertical comic about guinea pig sleep.

Across the cage...

A few hours later...

Fluffles Fact

Guinea pigs do not sleep all night like you. They take lots of short naps, day and night, and sometimes they even nap with their eyes open!

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. Nighttime. A clock says 3 A.M. Moonlight through the window. Fluffles is munching hay.

    Fluffles: Midnight snack time!

  2. Panel 2. Biscuit sits perfectly still with eyes wide open... and Z's floating up.

    Fluffles: Biscuit? Are you awake?

  3. Panel 3. Close-up of Biscuit staring blankly, still snoring.

    Fluffles: Your eyes are open but you are SNORING.

  4. Panel 4. Biscuit wakes up, explaining calmly.

    Biscuit: We can nap with our eyes open. Also... it is 3 a.m.

  5. Panel 5. Under the moon, both buddies share a midnight hay snack.

    Biscuit: Well... since we are both up...

    Sound: munch

  6. Panel 6. Morning sunshine. Both guinea pigs are fast asleep as the Tall One says good morning.

    The Tall One: Good morning, piggies!

    Caption: Guinea pigs: wide awake at 3 a.m., fast asleep at breakfast.
