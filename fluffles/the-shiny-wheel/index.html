The Shiny Wheel

Fluffles finds a magazine picture of a hamster on a shiny wheel and wants one. Biscuit has a better idea: guinea pig zoomies!

Flufflesby Comics Come AliveNo. 12

© 2026 Comics Come Alive

Fluffles Fact

Exercise wheels and rolling balls are not safe for guinea pigs, because their backs are not built for them. Instead they need a roomy cage and safe floor time to run.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. Fluffles sees a magazine with a picture of a hamster wheel.

    Fluffles: I want one of THOSE!

  2. Panel 2. Biscuit kindly explains that wheels are for hamsters, not guinea pigs.

    Biscuit: Wheels are for hamsters. Our backs are not built for spinning.

  3. Panel 3. Fluffles looks a little disappointed.

    Fluffles: Then how do we go FAST?

  4. Panel 4. Fluffles and Biscuit race around the safe floor-time pen doing zoomies.

    Sound: ZOOMIES!
