The Cucumber Caper

A cucumber slice has vanished without a trace! Fluffles follows the clues in a six-panel newspaper mystery with a very crunchy twist.

Flufflesby Comics Come AliveNo. 9

© 2026 Comics Come Alive

Fluffles Fact

Cucumber is mostly water, so it is fine as a small snack sometimes. Leafy greens and veggies are everyday foods, but hay is always the main meal.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. An empty food bowl in the cage.

    Caption: 11:02 a.m. A cucumber slice was here.

    Caption: 11:04 a.m. Gone.

  2. Panel 2. Fluffles squints with a magnifying glass, very serious.

    Fluffles: A crime has been committed.

  3. Panel 3. Fluffles questions Biscuit, who is clearly mid-nap.

    Fluffles: Biscuit! Where were you at 11:03?

    Biscuit: Napping. Obviously.

  4. Panel 4. Through the magnifying glass: a few green crumbs.

    Caption: Clue: tiny crumbs. And... a crunchy smell.

  5. Panel 5. Fluffles realizes the trail of crumbs leads straight to their own whiskers.

    Fluffles: The crumbs lead to... ME?!

  6. Panel 6. Fluffles grins sheepishly. Biscuit rolls their eyes.

    Fluffles: I ate it while I was thinking. Case closed!

    Biscuit: Detective of the year.
