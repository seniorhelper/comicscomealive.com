Fluffles Meets Captain Kapow

Captain Kapow, the hero of Comics Come Alive, drops in to meet the most curious guinea pig in town and discovers Fluffles has a superpower too.

The Adventures ofFluffles
Fluffles Meets Captain Kapow

Fluffles Fact

Guinea pigs have a real-life superpower: baby guinea pigs, called pups, are born with fur, open eyes and teeth, and they can run around just hours after they are born!

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. Wide splash: Captain Kapow lands in the living room with a mighty KAPOW. Fluffles and Biscuit jump in surprise.

    Caption: One ordinary afternoon...

    Sound: KAPOW!

  2. Panel 2. Captain Kapow greets Fluffles, who is amazed.

    Captain Kapow: Greetings! I heard a hero lives here.

    Fluffles: Me?!

  3. Panel 3. Fluffles fires off question after question.

    Fluffles: Can you fly?

    Fluffles: Do capes taste good?

    Fluffles: Do you like hay?

  4. Panel 4. Captain Kapow laughs and announces that curiosity is Fluffles' superpower.

    Captain Kapow: So many questions! THAT is your superpower: CURIOSITY!

  5. Panel 5. Fluffles wears a tiny red cape, beaming with pride.

    Fluffles: I have a SUPERPOWER?!

  6. Panel 6. Biscuit asks for a superpower and happily accepts the answer: naps.

    Biscuit: Do I get a superpower too?

    Captain Kapow: Yours is naps.

    Biscuit: Correct.

  7. Panel 7. Captain Kapow and caped Fluffles strike a heroic pose together.

    Captain Kapow: Stay curious, Fluffles!

    Fluffles: I will! What does "stay" mean?

    Caption: The end of Season One... for now!
