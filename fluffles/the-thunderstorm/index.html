The Thunderstorm

KRAKOOM! A big storm rolls in and two little guinea pigs learn that being scared is easier when you are scared together.

Then Biscuit scooted over...

By morning...

Fluffles Fact

Guinea pigs feel braver together. During loud storms or fireworks, a buddy, a hidey house and a calm, quiet room help them feel safe.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. Lightning flashes outside the window and thunder booms. Both guinea pigs jump in fright.

    Sound: KRAKOOM!

  2. Panel 2. Two wide eyes peek out of a dark hidey house.

    Fluffles: Biscuit? Are you scared?

  3. Panel 3. Biscuit comes over, a little nervous too, offering to stay close.

    Biscuit: A little. Let's be scared together. It's less scary that way.

  4. Panel 4. The Tall One drapes a soft blanket over the top of the cage and sits nearby, speaking softly.

    The Tall One: I'm right here. You're safe.

  5. Panel 5. Snuggled side by side, the storm softens to a gentle pitter patter.

    Fluffles: This is not so bad.

    Sound: pitter patter

  6. Panel 6. Morning. A rainbow fills the window and both guinea pigs popcorn for joy.

    Sound: POPCORN!
