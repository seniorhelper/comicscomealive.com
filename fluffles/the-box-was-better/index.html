The Box Was Better

A fancy new hidey house arrives in the mail! But Fluffles and Biscuit discover that sometimes the very best present is the box it came in.

The Adventures ofFluffles
The Box Was Better

Fluffles Fact

Plain cardboard boxes, with no tape, staples or ink, make great free hideouts and chew toys. Guinea pigs love hidey spots with two doors.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

  1. Panel 1. A big cardboard box lands in the room. Fluffles and Biscuit stare.

    Caption: A package arrives!

    Sound: THUD

  2. Panel 2. The Tall One unpacks a shiny deluxe hidey house.

    The Tall One: Ta-da! A deluxe hidey house!

  3. Panel 3. Fluffles politely inspects the deluxe house.

    Fluffles: Very nice. Very... deluxe.

  4. Panel 4. Fluffles notices the empty cardboard box now has two little doors cut into it.

    Fluffles: But THIS has two doors!

  5. Panel 5. Fluffles and Biscuit zoom in one door and out the other.

    Sound: ZOOM

  6. Panel 6. Both guinea pigs nap in the cardboard box. The deluxe house sits unused, full of hay.

    Biscuit: Next time, we just want the box.

    Caption: The deluxe house is very nice for storing hay.
