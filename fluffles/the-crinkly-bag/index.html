The Crinkly Bag

Fluffles hears the most mysterious sound in the house: CRINKLE. A classic four-panel newspaper strip about the happiest noise a guinea pig knows.

Flufflesby Comics Come AliveNo. 2

© 2026 Comics Come Alive

Fluffles Fact

When a guinea pig hears a sound that means food, like a crinkly bag of hay, it often lets out a loud, happy squeal called a "wheek."

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. Fluffles freezes in the cage, ears up, as a CRINKLE sound echoes from off panel.

    Fluffles: What was that?

    Sound: CRINKLE

  2. Panel 2. A big hand rustles a paper bag. Fluffles squeals with excitement.

    Fluffles (shouting): WHEEEEK!

    Sound: CRINKLE CRINKLE

  3. Panel 3. A huge pile of fresh hay is dumped in the cage and Fluffles leaps into it.

    Sound: FWUMP!

  4. Panel 4. Only the tips of two black ears stick out of the hay pile. Muffled munching sounds come from inside.

    Fluffles: Best sound in the world.

    Sound: munch munch munch
