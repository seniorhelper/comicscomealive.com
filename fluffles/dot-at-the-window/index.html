Dot at the Window

A tiny ladybug named Dot lands on the window and tells Fluffles all about the garden outside. A sunny vertical comic about friendship and fresh grass.

Fluffles has never been outside...

Later...

Fluffles Fact

Guinea pigs love fresh grass, but it should come from a yard with no weed killer or chemicals, be rinsed first, and never come from a lawn mower bag.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. A tiny red ladybug lands on the outside of the window glass and says hello.

    Dot: Hello in there!

  2. Panel 2. Fluffles stands up on back legs at the window, bursting with questions.

    Fluffles: Who are you? What is outside? Is it ALL hay?

  3. Panel 3. Dot shows off the garden outside: green grass, clover and flowers in the sun.

    Dot: I'm Dot! Out here there is grass, and clover, and sunshine!

  4. Panel 4. Fluffles closes their eyes and daydreams about grass.

    Fluffles (thinking): Fresh grass... mmmm...

  5. Panel 5. The Tall One brings a handful of fresh, rinsed grass from a garden with no chemicals.

    The Tall One: Fresh from our no-spray garden. Rinsed and ready!

  6. Panel 6. Fluffles munches happily while Dot waves goodbye through the glass.

    Dot: Same time tomorrow?

    Fluffles: Same time tomorrow!
