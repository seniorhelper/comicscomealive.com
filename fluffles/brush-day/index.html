Episode 18Motion comic · Animated, tap to play

Brush Day

The Tall One has a strange, bristly gadget and Fluffles is suspicious. An animated motion comic that ends in the happiest purr ever.

Tap the panel or press Next to turn the page. Arrow keys work too.

Fluffles Fact

A low, soft purr with a relaxed body usually means a guinea pig is content. Smooth-coated guinea pigs need only gentle brushing now and then; long-haired ones need it every day.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. A hand holds a soft pet brush. Fluffles squints at it suspiciously.

    Fluffles: What is that spiky thing?

  2. Panel 2. Fluffles backs into a corner, pretending to be busy.

    Fluffles: I am very busy. Very, very busy.

  3. Panel 3. The brush gently glides along Fluffles' back. Fluffles' eyes go wide.

    Sound: brush... brush...

  4. Panel 4. Fluffles melts into a happy, relaxed purr as hearts float up.

    Sound: purrrrr

  5. Panel 5. Fluffles requests more brushing. Biscuit lines up for a turn.

    Fluffles: Do the other side, please.

    Biscuit: And then me!
