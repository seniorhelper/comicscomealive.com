Popcorn Lessons

Biscuit can do a perfect popcorn hop. Fluffles cannot... yet. An animated motion comic about practice, patience, and one very bouncy surprise.

Tap the panel or press Next to turn the page. Arrow keys work too.

Fluffles Fact

"Popcorning" is when a happy guinea pig suddenly hops straight up, sometimes twisting in the air. It usually means they feel safe and excited.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. Biscuit hops straight up into the air. Fluffles watches, amazed.

    Fluffles: Whoa! How do you DO that?

    Sound: BOING!

  2. Panel 2. Biscuit explains how to popcorn.

    Biscuit: It's called popcorning. Step one: feel happy. Step two: jump!

  3. Panel 3. Fluffles scrunches up, trying very hard to jump. Nothing happens.

    Sound: hnnngh

  4. Panel 4. Fluffles flops down with a wobble, discouraged. Biscuit offers kind advice from off panel.

    Fluffles: I think I am broken.

    Biscuit: You can't force happy. Just have fun.

    Sound: wobble

  5. Panel 5. A hand arrives with a crinkly veggie bag. Fluffles perks up.

    Fluffles: Is that... a veggie bag?

    Sound: CRINKLE

  6. Panel 6. Fluffles pops straight up in a perfect popcorn hop. Biscuit smiles.

    Sound: POPCORN!

    Biscuit: Told you.
