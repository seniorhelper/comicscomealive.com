The Mysterious Tunnel

There is a brand new tunnel in the cage, and it is very, very dark inside. Brave Fluffles scrolls in to find out what is waiting at the other end.

Deeper...

Deeper still...

Fluffles Fact

In the wild, guinea pigs are prey animals, so they feel safest when they have places to hide. Every guinea pig should have its own hidey house or tunnel.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. A blue fabric tunnel appears in the cage. Fluffles eyes it with great curiosity.

    Fluffles: A tunnel! Where does it go?

  2. Panel 2. Only the black-and-white back end of Fluffles is visible, disappearing into the tunnel.

    Fluffles (whispering): Here I go...

  3. Panel 3. Darkness inside the tunnel. Two glowing eyes blink in the dark.

    Fluffles: It is so dark in here.

    Fluffles: ...and something is looking at me.

  4. Panel 4. Close-up of Fluffles, eyes huge, gulping.

    Sound: GULP

  5. Panel 5. The glowing eyes belong to Biscuit, comfortably napping in the middle of the tunnel.

    Biscuit: Oh. Hey, Fluffles. I live here now. It's cozy.

  6. Panel 6. Fluffles pops out the far end, popcorning with joy, while Biscuit pokes out the other side.

    Fluffles: A TUNNEL with a FRIEND in it? Best tunnel ever!
