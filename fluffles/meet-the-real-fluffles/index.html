Meet the Real Fluffles

A real photo of Fluffles the guinea pig comes to life in a scrapbook and pops into the cartoon world, ready to ask a million questions.

The Adventures ofFluffles
Meet the Real Fluffles

Fluffles Fact

Guinea pigs are not pigs, and they do not come from Guinea! They are rodents, also called cavies, and their wild cousins live in the grassy Andes mountains of South America.

Fluffles Facts are checked against guinea pig care guides from animal welfare groups and vets. For your own pet, always ask your vet.

Read the transcript

  1. Panel 1. A scrapbook page with a taped-in photo of the real Fluffles, a black guinea pig with a white band around the middle, eating greens from a lime green bowl in a cage.

    Caption: This is a real guinea pig named Fluffles.

    Caption: Black coat. White belt. A white stripe on top of the head.

  2. Panel 2. The same photo begins to sparkle. A tiny sniffing sound comes from it.

    Narrator: Wait. Did that photo just... sniff?

    Sound: sniff

  3. Panel 3. POP! A cartoon version of Fluffles bursts right out of the photo, eyes sparkling.

    Fluffles: Hi! Is this a comic?

    Sound: POP!

  4. Panel 4. Cartoon Fluffles faces the reader in a cage with a lime green bowl, introducing themself.

    Fluffles: I'm Fluffles! Black coat, white belt, and a white stripe on my head. Like a tiny headband.

  5. Panel 5. Fluffles stares curiously at the edge of the comic panel, question marks floating.

    Fluffles: Ooh. What is past this line?

  6. Panel 6. Fluffles stands up on back legs, delighted, peeking over to the next panel.

    Fluffles: More panels! More things to sniff!

  7. Panel 7. Wide panel of the whole cartoon cage: a green ramp, a hidey house and a hay bowl. Fluffles popcorns with joy.

    Fluffles: Welcome to my world. I have SO many questions!

    Caption: Next: The Crinkly Bag
