Short bedtime stories for kids, free to read online

The Sparkles Family is Mom Stephany, Dad Zach, big brother Coburn and Princess Phoenix, plus a kitten named Marshmallow who likes crowns a little too much. Each story is five short pages with big pictures and simple words, perfect for bedtime with kids ages 2 to 8.

After story time, visit Phoenix in her 3D princess castle, or play the kids learning games tomorrow morning.

Story time questions

Are these bedtime stories free to read online?

Yes. All of the Sparkles Family bedtime stories are free to read in your browser, with no sign-up and no ads.

What ages are the bedtime stories for?

They are written for kids about 2 to 8 years old. Each story is five pages long and takes about three minutes to read aloud.

How does Read to me work?

Read to me uses the text-to-speech voice built into your phone, tablet or computer. Each word lights up as it is read, which helps early readers follow along.
