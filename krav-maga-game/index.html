Krav Maga Game: Kapow's Dojo

Bow in, train on the bag, then spar the Kapow-Bot or a friend. Clean point sparring with jabs, crosses, kicks, blocks and dodges.

Sponsored Dojo sponsored by Do-Krav Combatives, Westminster, CO

Belt: White

Welcome to the dojo! Walk around, say hi to Sensei David, then pick a mode above.

Player 1 keys

Move A D   Jab F   Cross G

Front kick R   Round kick T   Block S   Dodge W

Playing solo? Arrow keys and J K I O work too.

Player 2 keys

Move ← →   Jab J   Cross K

Front kick I   Round kick O   Block ↓   Dodge ↑

On a phone or tablet, Player 1 uses the left buttons and Player 2 the right.

How point sparring works

Bow in, bow out

Every match starts and ends with a bow. Respect first, always. Sensei calls "Hajime!" to begin.

Scoring

Punches score 1 point and kicks score 2. A block or a dodge stops the point. First to 5 wins the match.

Belts

Finish bag training rounds to earn belts from white to black. Your belt is saved on this device.

Dojo questions

Is Kapow's Dojo a real Krav Maga game?

It is a family-friendly point sparring game inspired by Krav Maga and karate training. You can practice jabs, crosses, front kicks, round kicks, blocks and dodges against a bot or a friend.

Can two people play on one device?

Yes. Choose 2 players. On a keyboard, Player 1 uses the letter keys and Player 2 uses the arrow keys and J, K, I, O. On a phone or tablet, each player gets their own side of the screen.

Where can I take real Krav Maga classes near Denver?

The dojo in this game is sponsored by Do-Krav Combatives, a KAPAP Krav Maga school at 9165 Lowell Blvd in Westminster, Colorado. Call 720-583-4977 or visit mmatrainingdenver.com.
