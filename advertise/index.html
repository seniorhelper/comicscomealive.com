Advertising questions

How much does a custom branded comic cost?

Every project is quoted individually based on the format, length and level of animation or 3D work. Tell us about your goals and we will send a custom quote.

Is sponsored content labeled?

Yes. Every sponsored strip, comic and 3D world is clearly labeled as sponsored, and all content stays family friendly.

Can the comic use my real products and links?

Yes. Custom comics can feature your real product images, logo and links to your website or store, and you approve everything before it goes live.
