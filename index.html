Questions, answered

Is Comics Come Alive free?

Yes. Every comic, 3D world and learning game on the site is free to read and play, with no account needed.

Is it safe for young kids?

Yes. Everything is family friendly, there are no ads in the kids sections, and we do not collect information from kids. The optional face swap photo stays on your device and is never uploaded.

Do I need a VR headset for the 3D worlds?

No. The 3D worlds work on phones, tablets and computers. If you visit on a WebXR headset browser, a VR button appears so you can step inside.

Who makes these comics?

Every character and story is original and made for this site. Comics Come Alive is powered by Eye To Ad Media, a Denver-headquartered marketing and web studio.

Can my business get its own comic?

Yes. We create custom branded comics, sponsored strips and sponsored 3D worlds. Sponsored content is always clearly labeled. Visit the Advertise page to start.
