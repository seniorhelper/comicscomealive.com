Kids Learning Games

A 3D toy island full of learning games for ages 1 to 7. Tap, explore, and collect stars with Captain Kapow.

★ 0

Hi friend! Pick a game up top, or walk around and tap things!

How to play: tap a game button above to jump there. Tap flowers, ducks, balloons and animals. Walk with the joystick, arrow keys or WASD. Drag to look around. Explore mode is for little ones (every tap is a win). Learn mode asks questions for bigger kids.

For grown-ups: Puts your child's face on their character. The photo stays on this device and is never uploaded.

Free educational games for kids, built for little hands

Kapow's Toybox Island is a free 3D learning world for toddlers, preschoolers and early elementary kids. There are no ads, no sign-ups and no chat. Big buttons and a gentle guide make it easy for a 2-year-old, and Learn mode gives 5, 6 and 7-year-olds real questions to answer.

Color Garden

Find the red flower, the blue flower, the purple flower. Early color recognition for ages 1 to 4.

Counting Pond

Count the swimming ducks and tap the right number. Counting 1 to 5 for preschoolers.

Shape Blocks

Find the star, the ball, the cone and the heart. Shape names in 3D you can walk around.

ABC Balloons

Pop the letters in order from A to Z, with a word for every letter. Letter recognition for kindergarten.

Music Rainbow

A giant xylophone to tap or play along with. Music and patterns for every age.

Animal Friends and the Kindness Tree

Meet friendly animals, then pick hearts from the Kindness Tree for a kind idea to try today.

Parents ask

What ages are these learning games for?

Kapow's Toybox Island is made for kids ages 1 to 7. Explore mode suits toddlers and 2 to 3 year olds, and Learn mode asks color, counting, shape and letter questions for ages 4 to 7.

Are these kids learning games really free?

Yes. The games are free to play in your browser with no account, no ads in the kids world, and no downloads.

Does the face swap upload my child's photo?

No. The photo is taken and stored only on your own device, and it is never sent to our servers. You can remove it any time with the Remove photo button.

Does it work on a tablet or phone?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets, laptops and VR headsets. Kids can tap the big game buttons to jump straight to each game.
