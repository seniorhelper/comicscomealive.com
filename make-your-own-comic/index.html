Comic maker questions

Is this comic maker free?

Yes. You can make and download as many comic strips as you like for free, with no account.

Can I print my comic?

Yes. Press Download my comic to save a picture file, then print it or share it like any other image.

Do you pay for comic ideas?

No. The idea form is for grown-ups who want to share ideas for free. Businesses that want a custom comic can hire us through the Advertise page.
