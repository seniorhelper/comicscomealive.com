VR Comics: Step Inside the Story

3D choose-your-own-adventure comics. Walk around, look for clues, and pick what happens next. Your choices change the ending.

Clues: 0 / 3

The office. Midnight. Somebody ate my yogurt. Sweep the flashlight around and tap anything suspicious.

How to play: you are Frank Malone, private eye. Your flashlight points wherever you walk. Walk with the joystick, arrow keys or WASD, and tap glowing things to inspect them. Find 3 clues, question the suspects, then solve the case.

Captain Kablam wants to visit the moon. It's a long way up. How should he get there?

How to play: pick a choice at the bottom of the scene. Walk around each stop with the joystick or arrow keys. There are 27 different ways to reach an ending.

Interactive comics you can walk into

These VR comics turn classic comic panels into 3D scenes. In The Yogurt Job, a hard-boiled detective sweeps his flashlight across a midnight breakroom looking for the yogurt thief. In Kablam's Sky Adventure, a very determined superhero tries to reach the moon, and you decide how. Both work on phones, tablets, computers and WebXR VR headsets.

Prefer flat comics? The original strips live on the animated comics page, and the Kapow's Dojo sparring game is next door.

VR comics questions

What is a VR comic?

A VR comic is a comic story you can step inside. Instead of reading panels, you walk around a 3D scene, look for details and choose what happens next.

Do I need a VR headset?

No. These 3D comics run in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers. On a WebXR headset browser, a VR button appears so you can view the scene in virtual reality.

Are these choose your own adventure comics kid friendly?

Yes. The Yogurt Job is a silly office mystery with no violence, and Kablam's Sky Adventure is made for all ages.
