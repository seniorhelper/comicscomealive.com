Local Robot Makes Toast; Toast Achieves Orbit

Residents looked up at breakfast time to spot a single slice of lightly browned toast circling the moon. Bolt, the robot responsible, called the launch "a partial success" and has since switched to cereal.

Experts say the toast should return to Earth by next Tuesday, or whenever it feels like it.
