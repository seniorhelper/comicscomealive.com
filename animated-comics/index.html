Motion comics, the old-school way

These are original animated comic strips drawn in the classic newspaper style: halftone dots, thick ink borders and hand-lettered speech bubbles. Panels ink in one at a time, sound effects punch off the page, and some strips let you take part. Make a little dragon sneeze, send the text you definitely should not send, or sweep a flashlight across a crime scene.
